## section: refund-processing
### Refund processing

After a demo-store return is received and inspected, refunds are described as posting to the **original payment method within 5 to 10 business days**. This document explains the fictional timeline only. It does **not** authorize, promise, or execute a refund for any customer. Refund requests always require human review before any action.
