## section: eligibility-window
### Return eligibility and window

For the fictional SupportFlow demo store, most unused items may be returned within **30 calendar days** of the delivery date shown on the simulated order record. Items must be in original condition with tags attached and original packaging when practical. This demo policy does not decide eligibility for any individual order; a human reviews edge cases.

## section: return-process
### Return process

To start a demo-store return: (1) contact support with your order ID, (2) wait for a return authorization note from a teammate, (3) ship the item using the provided label within **7 days** of receiving that note, and (4) keep the carrier receipt until the return is recorded. Do not ship items without authorization in this demo workflow.

## section: non-returnable
### Non-returnable items

The following are **not returnable** under this fictional demo policy: gift cards, items marked final sale at purchase, opened personal-care products, and downloadable or activated software. Damaged items caused by misuse are also excluded. Questions about exceptions require human review.
